How we carry it

From pickup to handover

  1. Set up a business account and your regular lanes.
  2. Choose monthly or annual hiring, or a dedicated vehicle.
  3. Bookings under a contract use its agreed pricing and standards.
  4. A fixed truck and driver are used where possible.
  5. Multi-stop runs deliver to several stores in one trip.
  6. Reports show on-time performance and temperature records.
Safety rules

What we do, and never do

We always

  • Agree a rate card up front
  • Use a dedicated vehicle for steady lanes
  • Label every stop on multi-drop runs
  • Review delivery performance

We never

  • Mix incompatible cargo on the same run
  • Skip handover records
  • Rely on phone calls to track a trip
  • Leave the credit terms unchecked
