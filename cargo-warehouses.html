How we carry it

From pickup to handover

  1. Tell us the lanes, volumes and temperature bands.
  2. We match a verified, hygiene-certified reefer.
  3. Pallets are loaded in the order of the unloading stops.
  4. Temperature is logged against each stop.
  5. Door discipline keeps cold air in at every drop.
  6. Records support audits and disputes.
Safety rules

What we do, and never do

We always

  • Plan loading order around the drops
  • Keep each temperature band in its own compartment
  • Record temperature at every dock
  • Share live tracking with the receiving store

We never

  • Leave the dock door open during transfers
  • Load past the weight or compartment limit
  • Mix incompatible categories
  • Accept a truck with expired documents
