How we carry it

From pickup to handover

  1. Reefer is pre-cooled and the chilled setpoint is confirmed.
  2. Cargo is loaded on pallets with airflow left around it.
  3. Live readings stream to you from the compartment sensor.
  4. Route and ETA are shared with the receiver.
  5. Doors stay closed except at each drop.
  6. Receiver confirms delivery and the handover temperature is saved.
Safety rules

What we do, and never do

We always

  • Hold a steady chilled band
  • Leave airflow around pallets
  • Separate dairy from meat and seafood
  • Share live tracking with your receiver

We never

  • Combine with meat or seafood without a sealed, separate compartment
  • Let strong odours reach dairy
  • Skip the pre-cooling check
  • Leave the door open at a drop
